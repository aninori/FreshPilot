      How it gets there

      Three things change for the store team

      Orders match real demand

      Forecasts plan for the likely range of sales, not a single guess, and weigh the cost of waste against the cost of an empty shelf for every item.

      Problems are caught before they cost money

      Agents spot shelves that are probably empty, bad deliveries, and messy item data, and flag them the same day.

      People decide what matters

      Routine orders go through automatically. Managers see only a handful of calls a day, each with a clear reason.

      Measured on 8 weeks of simulated store data, compared with a simple rule (order last week's sales minus stock on hand).
